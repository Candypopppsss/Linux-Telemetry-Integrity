# Code of conduct

Participants must communicate respectfully, separate evidence from inference,
and critique technical claims without personal attacks.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and publication of
another person's private information are prohibited.

Use authorized research environments. Do not submit production credentials,
private telemetry, or third-party exploitation results without authorization.

Report conduct concerns through GitHub's private reporting mechanisms or an
available private maintainer contact. Do not post sensitive allegations in
public issues. Maintainers must recuse themselves from complaints involving
their own conduct.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, request corrections, limit participation,
or exclude participants. Enforcement should be proportional and consistent.
